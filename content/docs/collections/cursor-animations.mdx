A demo reads as a demo when something is being *used*. A cursor that walks to a
button and presses it does that in about forty frames, and it is the one thing a
static screenshot cannot fake.

[Cursor Track](/docs/screens/cursor-track) is the general case: give it waypoints
over any children and it moves, eases, and presses. The others are scenes with a
click already choreographed into them — [Roster Grant](/docs/scenes/roster-grant)
clicks a call to action and switches a team on,
[Tap Through](/docs/screens/tap-through) is the phone version.

Keep the path short. A pointer that wanders is a pointer the viewer is watching
instead of the product.
