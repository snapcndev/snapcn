Some beats are not about one screen, they are about *how much* there is: a
portfolio, a template library, a feed. These arrange your images in space and
move the camera through them.

[Orbit Gallery](/docs/scenes/orbit-gallery) rings them around a centre mark,
[Moodboard Reveal](/docs/scenes/moodboard-reveal) flies a scatter in and lands on
a hero, and [Card Rail](/docs/scenes/card-rail) flicks them sideways on a tilted
plane. All of them take your own images — pass real ones, because a gallery
running snapcn's demo pictures is a video about snapcn.
