The plain-language terms for each way of using snapcn. The videos you render are
always yours — no attribution, no royalty, on any plan.

## Free components — MIT

Every free component is MIT licensed, the same as the
[repository](https://github.com/snapcndev/snapcn). Use them anywhere, for anything,
including client work.

## Pro and Lifetime — your own work

Pro (annual) and Lifetime cover **one person** using the Pro components, the
templates and the snapcn MCP server in videos for **their own products and
projects** — including ones that make money.

They do not cover **client work**: videos you make for someone else who pays you
for them. That is what Commercial is for.

## Commercial — client and company work

Commercial covers everything in Lifetime, licensed for **client and company work**,
for **five people**: you, and up to four teammates you invite from your
[account page](/account). Each teammate signs in with the address you invited,
gets Pro and their own API keys, and loses both the moment you remove them.

## On every paid plan

- **All sales are final. There are no refunds** — try any component free in the
  gallery before you buy.
- The source is copied into your project and stays yours — an annual plan that
  stops renewing does not take back what you installed.
- You may not resell or redistribute the Pro source itself — as a component
  library, a template pack, or anything else whose product is the code rather
  than a video.

## Bought by 30 September 2026

Pro or Lifetime paid for by 30 September 2026 (23:59 anywhere on Earth) carries
the Commercial terms — client work, and four teammates — at no extra cost. It is
already on your account: the Team section of your account page is where you
invite them.

Questions about a specific use: reply to your purchase email.
