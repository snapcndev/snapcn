---

Every launch video reaches the beat where a number has to land: installs this week, revenue, time saved. A screenshot of a dashboard reads as a still. These draw the chart on the frame clock instead, so the number arrives as part of the motion rather than sitting on top of it.

They are plain Remotion components — `useCurrentFrame()` and `interpolate()`, no chart library — so they render frame-exact to MP4, and the labels, totals and series are props: the numbers on screen are yours.

Everything in this category is part of [snapcn Pro](/docs/pricing?ref=charts) — and one Pro component is free with your email. Each card opens the component's own page, with the full demo, how it moves, and how it installs.
